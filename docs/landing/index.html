Features

Everything you need to manage your music library

Built for the terminal. Powered by Last.fm and AI. Fast, beautiful, and open source.

⭐

10-Point Ratings

Rate albums, album tracks, and standalone songs on a precise 1–10 star scale. Update ratings anytime.

🏆

Tier Lists

Create S/A/B/C/D/E tier lists with album cover art. Export as full-resolution PNG images with artist watermarks.

🤖

AI Reviews

Generate personalised critic reviews contextualised by your rating. Uses GPT-4o-mini or local Ollama.

💬

AI Chat

Chat with your music library. "Which of my albums would work for studying?" Answer: instant.

📤

Export Anywhere

Export ratings as CSV for spreadsheets, Markdown for blog posts, or a self-contained HTML page for GitHub Pages.

📊

Stats Dashboard

Average ratings, top album, artist breakdown, and total track counts — your music taste at a glance.

🎨

Beautiful TUI

Rich tables, coloured panels, spinners, and emoji — gorgeous in any modern terminal. Works with --no-color too.

💾

Smart Storage

Data lives in your OS user directory (not the current folder). Auto-migrates legacy albums.json from v1.

🔒

Privacy-First

All data stays local. Use --local for Ollama AI to keep everything on your machine with zero cloud calls.

AI Features

musicli is AI-first in 2026

Every AI command works with OpenAI or a local Ollama model. Your data, your choice.

📝 AI Review

Generate a critic-quality review personalised by your rating.


musicli ai review "Radiohead" "OK Computer" --rating 10

💡 Recommendations

Get 5–7 personalised album recommendations from your taste profile.


musicli ai recommend

🏆 Describe Tier List

AI narrates what your tier list reveals about your taste in music.


musicli ai describe-tier "My Ranking"

🎤 Compare Artists

Side-by-side AI analysis of two artists from your own ratings.


musicli ai compare "Radiohead" "Nirvana"

📊 Monthly Digest

A friendly written summary of your listening stats and patterns.


musicli ai digest

💬 Library Chat

Free-form conversation with your music library as context (RAG).


musicli ai chat

🏷️ Auto-Tag

Tag any album with mood, genre, era, and thematic labels.


musicli ai tag "Radiohead" "Kid A"

🖥️ Local AI (Ollama)

Run all AI features offline with a local model. No API key needed.


musicli ai recommend --local
